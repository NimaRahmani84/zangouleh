Enroll & Pay — ثبت‌نام

قوانین و الزامات · Requirements

Please Read Before Enrolling

💳 Payments
Tuition is accepted by cash or Interac e-Transfer to zangoulehmusicschool@gmail.com, and must be paid in full before the first session of each term. Pricing appears below once you select a class type.
📅 Absence & Classes
No session may be cancelled by the student, but it may be replaced with an online session at the same class time — otherwise it is forfeited. This does not apply to Children's Group classes: missed sessions there cannot be rescheduled unless parents conduct the session at home per the instructor's guidance. Students registered for a full 12-session term may place classes on hold for 4–6 sessions; held sessions carry over to the next term. Students should be ready 5 minutes before class starts.
🎥 Recording & Publishing
The school may record or photograph classes and activities for documentation or promotion on its social media. Students may indicate disapproval before signing; otherwise, signing implies consent to recording and publication.
🔒 Security & Privacy
The academy is equipped with 8 security cameras recording audio and video for the safety of students and instructors. The school safeguards this information but is not responsible for the release of any portion of it.
🌿 Allergies & Health Notification (Children's Classes)
Parents/guardians must inform the school of any food or environmental allergies and specific medical conditions that may affect their child's health and safety during class. Failure to provide this information exempts the school from responsibility in this regard.
💊 Medication & Health Conditions
Parents/guardians must provide complete information about any health conditions or need for specific medications during class. The school bears no responsibility for inaccurate information or non-adherence to medical requirements — full responsibility falls on the parents.
